The morning rush is on! Take orders, mix, bake, top and serve — before the customers lose patience and walk out.

How to play
1️⃣ Click an ingredient on the counter to put it on your tray.
2️⃣ Click the tray item, then a station: Mixer makes dough & batter, Oven bakes it, Prep Board readies toppings, Coffee Bar brews.
3️⃣ Collect finished food from the station, click the plate to add it.
4️⃣ When the plate matches an order, the ticket glows — click it (or Serve) to cash in. Fast serves pay tips; quick serves in a row build a combo.
⚠️ Food left in the oven too long burns — click it to trash it.
⌨️ Keys: 1–7 pick ingredients · Space serves · T trash · P pause · M mute.
